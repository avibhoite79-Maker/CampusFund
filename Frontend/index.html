CampusFund

Funds stay locked until donors say the work is done.

CampusFund holds every donation inside an Ethereum smart contract and hands it to the organiser one milestone at a time.

  1. DonateYour ETH goes into the contract, not to the organiser.
  2. VoteDonors approve each milestone. A bigger donation counts for more.
  3. ReleaseThe organiser gets that milestone's share. Goal missed? Donors get refunds.

Campaigns

Outer ring: one segment per milestone. Inner ring: how much of the goal is funded.
